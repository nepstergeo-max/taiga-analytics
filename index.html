TAIGA Analytics

TAIGA Analytics is a private, internal tool used by its owner to manage and analyse the owner's own YouTube channels.

It uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to:

  • read channel and video statistics and analytics reports (views, traffic sources, subscribers, revenue) of the owner's channels;
  • check the publishing schedule and processing status of the owner's uploaded videos;
  • read and reply to comments on the owner's videos and remove abusive comments;
  • upload videos to the owner's channels.

The tool is not offered to the public. It is used only with YouTube channels that belong to its owner.
