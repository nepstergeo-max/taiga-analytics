Privacy Policy — TAIGA Analytics

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who uses this app

TAIGA Analytics is a private, internal tool. It is used only by its owner, with YouTube channels that belong to the owner. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, the app uses YouTube API Services to access, for the owner's own channels: channel and video information, YouTube Analytics reports (including revenue reports), comments on the owner's videos, and the ability to upload and manage the owner's videos.

How the data is used

The data is used only to analyse the performance of the owner's channels, check the publishing schedule of uploaded videos, answer and moderate comments, and upload the owner's videos. The app does not use the data for advertising and does not sell it.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and reports are stored only on the owner's own computer. The data is not shared with or transferred to any third party.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are then useless and are removed from the owner's computer.
